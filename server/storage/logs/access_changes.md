## 1. Access-Change Proposals (Policy Changes)
These items alter the seed role-to-permission mapping, grant or remove access for roles, or modify organizational scope semantics. Any widening item not required by existing test suites is isolated on branch `review/access-changes`.

| Route / Permission | Old Definition / Scope | New Proposed Definition | Roles Gaining | Roles Losing | Classification & Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `correspondence.issue_direct` | Seeded global permission assigned to `super_admin` | Deprecate and remove from reference vocabulary | None | `super_admin` | Policy change: Deprecated administrative bypass; all official outgoing correspondences in WU-DMS mandate institutional intake and dispatch registration workflows (`correspondence.register` and `correspondence.dispatch`). | `Document::allowsUser('archive.view')` | Strict departmental `owner_unit_id` scope | Cross-unit read operational custody for archived records to users holding `archive.view` | `archivist` | None | Policy decision: Applied on `audit/gap-fix`, awaiting approval. Authorized archivists holding `archive.view` in their authorized unit can read non-confidential archived records originated by other departments. Mutating operations (`archive.transfer`, `archive.request_disposal`, etc.) and confidential records without clearance remain strictly confined to the originating department. `hasConfidentialClearance` strictly requires `staff.view_sensitive` (removes `documents.delete` which is held by `admin` and `super_admin`). |
